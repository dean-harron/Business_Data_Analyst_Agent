# Analyst Metric Definitions

## Revenue
Revenue is the total recognized sales value over the chosen period. Always confirm whether the source is gross revenue, net revenue, booked revenue, or recognized revenue.

## Gross margin
Gross margin percentage = (revenue - cost of goods sold) / revenue * 100. Margin must be interpreted relative to accounting definitions and the relevant period.

## Customer growth
Customer growth compares the number of customers between comparable periods. Report the absolute change and the percentage change.

## Marketing ROI
Marketing ROI depends on the chosen attribution model. Do not claim causality from simple correlations between marketing spend and revenue.

## Engagement rate
For social media, engagement rate can be defined in multiple ways. Always state numerator, denominator, and time window. Common numerators include likes, comments, shares, and saves.

## YouTube views
Public YouTube video statistics can include view count, like count, and comment count. Private channel analytics such as watch time and revenue require appropriate authorized access rather than only public channel data.

## Sports efficiency
Sports metrics are sport-specific. Never mix denominators or definitions across sports. State the formula and season context before comparing players.
