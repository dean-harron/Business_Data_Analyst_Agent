# Sports Analytics Guidance

Sports analysis should start with the sport, competition, season, player role, minutes/possessions/opportunities, and the exact metric definitions. Rate statistics are usually more comparable than raw totals when exposure differs.

Professional workflow:
1. validate identifiers and seasons
2. inspect missingness and sample sizes
3. normalize for exposure where appropriate
4. compare to relevant baselines
5. separate descriptive results from causal claims
6. report uncertainty and small-sample warnings
7. recommend actions that match the coaching or scouting question
